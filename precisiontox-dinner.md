In June 2022, I helped organize an informal dinner for more than 20 people as part of the general assembly meeting of the [PrecisionTox consortium](https://precisiontox.org/) — a gathering bringing together researchers from across the project's partner institutions. This required extensive liasing with the owner of Bar Cal Chusco in order to host the dinner at a short notice.
