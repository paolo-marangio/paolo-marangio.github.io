## Growing the MSP Alumni WhatsApp Community {#whatsapp-community}

As a volunteer, pro bono effort within the same alumni network, I grew the MSP Alumni WhatsApp community from 0 to more than 400 members, structured across several country-specific group chats to keep conversations relevant and manageable at that scale.
